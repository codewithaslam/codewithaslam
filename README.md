## 💭 A bit about me

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science student who spends more time building things than the syllabus probably expects. I work across the stack — from training models to shipping the app that serves them — because I like owning a project end to end rather than just one layer of it.

Right now I'm deep in **Computer Vision and LLMs**, figuring out how to take them from "cool demo" to "actually useful product." Outside of code, I lead design for **IEEE SB AISAT** and represent **Google as a Student Ambassador** on campus.

- 🔭 Currently building AI-powered tools people can actually use day to day
- 🌱 Learning: advanced deep learning architectures, agentic LLM workflows
- 🏆 Hackathon winner — I like building under pressure
- 💬 Ask me about: computer vision pipelines, full-stack architecture, or how to actually finish a side project
- ⚡ Fun fact: I'd rather debug a model at 2am than leave a bug unsolved overnight
